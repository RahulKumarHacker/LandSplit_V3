Saving your work

“Save” downloads a project file (.json) to your computer – this is your real backup and can be re-opened any time. Your browser also keeps an automatic local copy as a convenience; it is separate from the downloaded file, is tied to this browser/device, and can be erased if you clear browser data.

Bihar land unit conversions

Local units differ between districts and even between villages. Enter the values that apply to your area (in square metres). The defaults are common approximations (1 bigha = 20 katha, 1 katha = 20 dhur, 1 katha ≈ 1361.25 ft²).

Create plot from sides & angles

Walk around the plot: enter each side's length and the interior angle at each corner (the angle inside the plot between the two sides). The last side and the last two angles are calculated for you – an n-sided plot needs 2n−3 measurements.
How to use LandSplit

1. Draw the plot

  • Draw boundary: click each corner in turn. Click the first point again, press Enter or double-click to close the plot. Backspace removes the last point, Esc cancels.
  • Parallelogram (P): press and drag (or click twice) to set the first side, then click a third point; the fourth corner is calculated so opposite sides are parallel and equal. Or type base, side and angle under “Quick … by size”.
  • Parallel lines are marked automatically: matching arrow marks (>, >>) on lines with the same direction and a ∥ badge between neighbouring ones. Turn it off, or add the distance between the lines, in the settings.
  • Rectangle / Square: press and drag on the canvas. Or type a width and length under “Quick rectangle by size”.
  • Names: corners are named A, B, C… and sides AB, BC… (or 1, 2, 3… under “Naming”). Tick or untick “Show corner names” and “Show side names” in the settings to show or hide them.
  • Lock a side length or corner angle: pick the Lock tool (K) and click a side to lock its length, or click a corner / its angle to lock that angle (click again to unlock). You can also tick “Lock” on a selected side or corner, or use the Locks panel. Locked values stay fixed while you drag corners or edit other values; the unlocked sides and angles stretch to fit.
  • Edit current plot… opens the plot you drew in the sides & angles editor so you can change any length or angle by typing, then “Update current plot”.
  • By typing: use “Enter sides & angles manually…” to type every side length and corner angle and get an exact polygon. Angles are shown live while you draw.

2. Edit it

  • Select: click a corner, side, dividing line or parcel. Drag corners to reshape. Drag empty space to pan; scroll or pinch to zoom.
  • Add point: click on a side to insert a corner. Move point: drag corners. Move line: drag a side (or a dividing line sideways).
  • Delete: click a corner to remove it, a side to collapse it, or a dividing line to remove it.
  • Select a side to type its actual length, or use it as a scale reference. Select a corner to type its interior angle. You can also set “1 grid square = …” under Units & scale.

3. Divide it

  • Divide line: click (or press-and-drag) from one point on the boundary to another. Ends snap to the boundary or to another dividing line. Repeat for more parcels.
  • Select a dividing line to move it by an exact distance, or drag it with Move line. Parcel areas, lengths and angles update live.

4. Save & share

  • Save / Save As download a project .json file; Open loads one so you can keep editing.
  • Export CSV gives lengths, angles, areas and perimeters. Print / PDF opens the print dialog – choose “Save as PDF”.
